Lila Congrove is a Master of Science student in the School of Criminology and Criminal Justice at the University of Nebraska at Omaha. Her research interests include police officer discretion, drug trafficking, and organized crime. Lila holds a B.S. in Criminology and Criminal Justice with minors in Psychology and Spanish from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. Currently, Lila is serving as a Graduate Assistant under the mentorship of Dr. Sadaf Hashimi.
